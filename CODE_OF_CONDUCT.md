# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

DraftApply contributors and maintainers must provide a welcoming, professional, harassment-free environment. Be respectful, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, focus discussion on the work, and protect users' personal and security-sensitive information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, publication of others' private information, and sustained disruptive behavior are unacceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile or, once published, the project's monitored contact. Do not disclose a reporter's identity without permission. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when behavior is inappropriate.

Until the owner publishes a monitored contact, GitHub's reporting and blocking tools are the available escalation path. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, and a consistent response to comparable conduct.
